Private by design

Your entries stay close.

WeightSpan has no user accounts and does not sync your core weight, profile, or reminder data to Chevelon Software. It sends limited feature-use analytics and technical crash and error diagnostics to PostHog to help improve reliability. Diagnostics may include technical error details, stack traces, app/build and device information, and anonymous event or session identifiers, but do not intentionally attach entered health data, free text, or identity. Client IP data is discarded, location is not inferred from IP addresses, session replay is off, and the app does not use advertising or cross-app tracking.
